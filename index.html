Yes. Open the live Fresha menu, select the service and artist you prefer, then choose from the available times shown there.

Start with the closest colour service in the menu. For a major change or a first vivid, ask the studio about a consultation before locking the service.

Do you work with curls and texture?+

Yes. The studio profile highlights the Curly Girl Method and curl-focused work. Choose a curly cut or texture service in the live menu.
